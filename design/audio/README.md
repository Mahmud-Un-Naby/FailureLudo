# Offline game audio

The `tabletop_*.wav` files are original procedural audition sounds generated by
[generate_tabletop.py](generate_tabletop.py), using only oscillators and seeded noise.
These original prototype files contain no third-party recordings. They are prototype candidates,
not the final recorded dice/pawn pack proposed in plan 010.

The dice impacts are placed at 50, 140, 240, 340, 460, and 540 ms to accompany the
600 ms roll. Movement contacts play at the end of each visible step. Sources are
retained so the pack can be regenerated or retuned. All assets are bundled offline.

Format: mono, 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV, with endpoint fades and peak headroom.
Audition on device before selecting the final sonic direction.

## Sound choices and adding options

Settings → Advanced settings contains the per-action sound lists, previews, and reset action.
These preferences apply to all games, persist on the device, and are independent of new-game
setup. Home and in-game settings share the same picker. All sounds are ordinary options
in [SoundCatalog.kt](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/failureludo/feedback/SoundCatalog.kt), with each category's default identified by ID. No legacy or audition assets are offered automatically.

To add a sound, bundle its file in `app/src/main/res/raw/` and add a `SoundOption` to the matching
category's `options` list with a unique, permanent ID, a player-facing label, its `R.raw` reference,
and suitable gain/pitch settings. Record its source/license here. The picker discovers the entry
automatically; no gameplay or preference-schema edits are required. Preserve IDs across file
or label renames. The category's `defaultId` must point to a member of its list. Missing, obsolete,
or wrong-category saved IDs resolve to that default.

Playback preloads only selected assets and loads other options on preview. Gameplay cues are
never queued while loading, so a late sample cannot produce an out-of-time action sound.
Previews wait for loading and respect the sound switch and master volume. Replaced assets are
unloaded when selections change or another preview is requested; sound pools are released when
the owning screen closes. Actual listening/device review remains a manual check.


## Recorded wooden alternatives — September 28

Four prepared recordings are bundled from CC0 sources. Source files, creators, exact download
URLs, license/version, retrieval date, SHA-256 hashes, trim times, and filter settings are in
[the source manifest](sources/wooden_options/manifest.json). The original recordings are retained
beside it. [prepare_wooden_options.py](prepare_wooden_options.py) regenerates the app WAV files
using NumPy, SciPy, and libsndfile; these are development tools, not app dependencies.

| Category | Option | Source | Prepared duration | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dice roll | Wooden tumble | Wuzzy, wooden die on wooden table, take 1 | 640 ms | Yes |
| Dice roll | Light wooden roll | Wuzzy, wooden die on wooden table, take 4 | 600 ms | No |
| Pawn movement | Wooden tap | Aidan_Walker, wobble04, first contact | 130 ms | No |
| Capture | Wooden double knock | Aidan_Walker, wobble01, first two contacts | 300 ms | No |

Sources: [Wuzzy's wooden dice](https://opengameart.org/content/wooden-dice-on-wodden-table-roll)
and [Aidan_Walker's boardgame piece wobbles](https://opengameart.org/content/boardgame-piece-wobbles).
Both source pages list [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), verified
2026-09-28. Credit is retained here even though attribution is not a CC0 condition.

The processing trims silence, removes low room rumble, softens high-frequency clicks, adds
short endpoint fades, and leaves peak headroom. Catalog gains approximately match the dice
and pawn alternatives to their previous cues by RMS level; perceived loudness still needs listening. The dice retain a 50 ms lead matching the prior
cue's first contact. No recording is time-stretched or synthesized. Each file is mono 22,050 Hz
16-bit PCM, ready for offline SoundPool playback. Device listening remains for user review;
these choices have been checked for timing/levels, not subjectively approved on a phone.

Wooden tumble is used for new/default preferences and after Reset sound choices. An already
saved original dice selection stays selected; choose Wooden tumble or reset in Advanced settings
to switch it. Original tabletop dice remains a list option with the same stable ID. The pawn
and capture defaults are unchanged; their wooden alternatives are optional.
